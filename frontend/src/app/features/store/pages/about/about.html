Our Story

A cup of tea built on honesty.

How We Started

Born in Gujarat.
Built on a question.

In 1986, our founder set up a small tea stall in Gujarat and asked a question nobody in the industry wanted to answer: why does the person who grows the tea know the least about where it ends up? The supply chain was long, murky, and unkind to the people doing the hardest work.

Rajhans Tea began as a direct response to that — a small operation buying leaves straight from farming families, testing them ourselves, and selling them with complete transparency about their origin. No middlemen. No auctions. No mystery.

What started as a conversation over a cup of tea has grown into a business that now serves hundreds of thousands of households across India. But the question that started it all — and the answer we committed to — has never changed.

What We Value

Three beliefs that run through everything we do.

01 — Purity

Nothing goes in
that shouldn't be there.

No artificial flavouring, no colouring, no filler. Just the leaf. We test every single batch before it leaves our facility — flavour, moisture, particle size, and freshness. If it doesn't meet our standard, it doesn't ship. It's not a rule we follow — it's the only way we know how to work.

02 — Fairness

The people who grow it
deserve to be seen.

We work directly with over 200 farming families across Assam. We pay above market rates, on time, every time. We've formally recognised over 3,000 tea pluckers through our Fair Pay Initiative — because without them, there is no Rajhans Tea. The economics of this business only make sense if they're good for everyone in it.

03 — Transparency

You should know exactly
where your tea comes from.

We built India's first full leaf-to-cup traceability system. Every pack of Rajhans Tea can be traced back to the specific garden and harvest it came from. We're not hiding anything — not the sourcing, not the price we paid, not the process. We believe transparency is the foundation of trust, and trust is the foundation of everything else.

How We Work

From the garden to your door.

Every step is handled with intention. Nothing is outsourced, nothing is rushed.

01

Garden Sourcing

We visit our partner gardens in Assam every harvest season. We cup the tea on-site, assess the soil, and only source what meets our standard. No blind auctions. Always direct.

02

Blending

Our master blender works with the raw leaf to create consistent blends batch after batch. Every tea is cupped blind before approval. The goal is always the same — a cup that tastes exactly as it should.

03

Packaging

We pack in small batches to preserve freshness. Every pouch is sealed immediately after packing. We don't stockpile finished product — we pack when an order comes in, so what reaches you is never stale.

04

Delivery

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of packing. We partner with reliable logistics providers and share tracking information with every order. Your tea arrives fresh, always.
